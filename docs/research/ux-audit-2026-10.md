## Correction to the baseline

The brief says "no bar stays pinned after scrolling; the 01-05 step pills scroll away." **That is wrong.** The step nav is `position: sticky; top: 0` (`src/components/App.tsx:246`) and stays pinned: its top edge measured 519, 219, 0, 0, 0 at scrollY 0/300/700/1200/1800 (desktop; phone 589, 289, 0). It is 61 px tall, 6.8% of the desktop viewport, inside the 10% guidance.
`shoot.mjs` misreports it because `html { scroll-behavior: smooth }` (`src/index.css:15`) makes `window.scrollTo()` animate, so the pinned-check runs before the page has moved. Fix the script with `scrollTo({top: h, behavior: 'instant'})` before trusting its "pinned" line on any app. The same bug may hide pinned bars in the other repos' baseline.
The real nav problems are different: see RPG-UX-5 and RPG-UX-9.

## Verdict

The tool is competent and unusually careful about accessibility, but it is one long scroll: 2.5 screens on a fresh desktop load, 3.4 on a phone, 4.6 and 7.6 after one sample run, and 4.2 and 6.2 (7.3 with a plan) once a roster is loaded. A 768 px column sits in the middle of a 1440 px screen, so wide screens add length instead of using the width. The first phone screen is a hero plus a small ghost button, with the sample buttons cut by the fold. Smaller bugs sit on top of that: the "scroll to results" jump lands in the wrong place, the phone nav hides the active step, and there is a real 320 px reflow failure.
The biggest single win is a layout change that is Natalie's call (Decision D1): put inputs and results side by side on wide screens and stop stacking five steps on phones.

## Decisions for Natalie

**D1. Page structure (blocks RPG-UX-1, 3).** It protects: a first-time visitor reaching a result without scrolling 4-7 screens. If left alone, every step added later makes the page longer.

1. Two columns from `lg` (inputs and the Optimise controls left and sticky; results, roster, teams and plan right), single column on phones with collapsible sections. Uses the width and keeps one URL and the scroll feel; the biggest layout rewrite.
2. Real tabs or a stepper (Load, Roster, Teams, Plan, Optimise; one visible at a time; a hash per tab so Back works). Each view is short and the nav becomes true navigation; it hides the other steps and loses the "one scrolling story" feel.
3. Keep the single column, but collapse finished or secondary sections (roster rows, teams, meta boxes) behind accordions and shrink the hero. Smallest change; the page stays long and the 768 px column stays narrow.

**D3. First viewport and hero copy (blocks RPG-UX-2).** It protects: the first action being on screen at 390x844, and the honesty of the "fixed demo" number (it is not the user's result).

1. Compact hero: h1, one line, and the two actions (Load gear, Try a sample) first; the 219.4 demo becomes a small caption or moves lower. Action first; loses the big proof number at the top.
2. Keep the big number but make it a real, labelled sample result with the sample buttons inside the hero. Keeps the strongest recruiter hook; a taller hero.
3. Keep the hero as is and only pull the sample panel above the step nav on phones. Smallest edit; the phone first screen is still mostly hero.

**D2. Branding (blocks RPG-UX-17).** It protects: a recognisable link preview (chat, LinkedIn) and a tab or home-screen icon. Today a link shared in chat shows only a text title.

1. Wordmark only: reuse the existing gold crosshair favicon as a small logo in the header, plus a generated OG card from the existing dark and gold palette. Cheapest; stays generic.
2. A proper mark and name treatment (you choose or commission the icon; an agent builds the header, favicon set, OG image and `site.webmanifest`). Most identity; needs a design decision and an asset.
3. Metadata only: add OG and Twitter tags with a screenshot of the sample result as the preview image, leave the header text-only. Fast; the app still has no logo.

Issue #100 (the OKLCH palette) is a separate owner decision and does not block anything above.

## What held up

Skip link, visible focus rings, `aria-disabled` rather than `disabled` on running buttons, `scroll-margin-top` on focusable elements (S-NAV-2 passes for anchors and Tab), reduced-motion handling, live regions, the bad-shared-link error state (clear text plus "Start Fresh"), Escape closes the character drawer, no horizontal scroll at 390, a designed progress state for the 8-solve plan, "Show all" reveals on the roster and the builds, and CLS and LCP within budget.
Not tested: the UID fetch (needs a real UID and a call to a third party), slow-network behaviour, screen readers.
